## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **AI Engineer** focused on building practical, production-oriented AI systems rather than isolated model experiments.

My work sits at the intersection of **machine learning, software engineering, and AI infrastructure**, with a particular focus on:

- 🎙️ **Speech AI** — ASR, TTS, translation and speech-to-speech systems
- 🎬 **Multimodal & Video AI** — video understanding, semantic analysis, reframing and automated content pipelines
- 🧠 **LLM Systems** — local LLMs, embeddings, RAG, retrieval and structured AI workflows
- ⚙️ **AI Infrastructure** — containerized inference, GPU workloads, databases and self-hosted services
- 🏗️ **AI System Architecture** — taking ML systems from prototype to deployable products

> I enjoy solving the engineering problems between **“the model works”** and **“the product works.”**
